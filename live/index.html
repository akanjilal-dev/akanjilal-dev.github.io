Connectors, quantum feed, and vendor health

A self refreshing view of the Model Context Protocol servers published by the major clouds, the latest quantum activity, and the current status of the main quantum vendors. The data is collected from official public sources by a scheduled job and baked into the site, so nothing is fetched from a third party in your browser.

Connectors

Model Context Protocol servers, read directly from the Amazon, Google, and Microsoft repositories.

Quantum feed

The latest releases and announcements from Amazon Braket, IBM Quantum, Google Quantum AI, and Microsoft Azure Quantum.

Vendor health

Public service status for the main quantum vendors. Where a vendor publishes a machine readable status feed it shows a live badge, otherwise it links the human status page. Device level availability needs credentials and is intentionally out of scope for a public site.
